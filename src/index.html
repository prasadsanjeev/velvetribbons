The signature finish

The art of
the unwrap.

  1. i.

    Paper, folded crisp

    Rich, heavyweight papers chosen for the occasion, with sharp hand-creased edges and hidden seams. No tape in sight.

  2. ii.

    Silk ribbon, tied true

    Velvet, silk and satin ribbons, wrapped taut and centred. Brand colours available for corporate orders.

  3. iii.

    The signature bow

    Our hand-tied bow: full loops, trimmed tails, and the kind of volume that makes people pause before they pull.

  4. iv.

    The final flourish

    Gift tags, wax seals or fresh trims, so the moment before it's opened feels as special as the gift.

How it works

From your hands
to theirs, beautifully.

Three simple steps. You bring the gift and the occasion; we handle every fold, knot and flourish.

I

Tell us the occasion

Share what you're gifting and the moment it's for. We'll suggest papers, ribbons and finishes that fit.

II

We wrap by hand

Crisp folds, silk ribbon, signature bow. Add gift tags, wax seals or fresh trims for the final flourish.

III

Pick up or delivery

Collect from our Whitby studio, or have finished gifts delivered anywhere in Durham Region and the GTA.
